Clip 01 How it works

Press the key after it happens.
Vice already has it.

Vice holds the last two minutes in memory, so you never have to remember to start recording before the good part. Press F9 and the last 20 seconds become a clip. Until you do, nothing is written to disk.

  1. Play or work. The graphics card does the encoding, usually for under 1% of your CPU.
  2. Press F9. The last 20 seconds save as a clip. Double-tap for a whole match.
  3. Keep it or send it. Trim it, edit it, file it in a playlist, or copy its link.

Clip 02 Sharing

Send a link, not a file.

Every clip gets a public link the moment it saves. Paste it into Discord and it plays inline. There is no Vice server and no upload: the link points at your own computer, so no company keeps a copy of your videos and there is no size limit.

  • Each link uses a random token, so one link never leads anyone to your other clips.
  • Right-click Share for a copy that fits Discord's free upload limit, ready to paste.
  • Delete the clip and its link stops working. You decide what stays online.

Links reach your computer through a Cloudflare tunnel, which passes the video along without storing it. Without cloudflared installed, links work on your own network.

Clip 03 Recording

Light enough to leave on all day.

Vice records with gpu-screen-recorder, which encodes on the graphics card: NVENC on NVIDIA, VAAPI on AMD and Intel. Your game keeps its frames.

<1%CPU while recording
144frames per second
AV1HEVC and H.264 too, in 10-bit
  • Game Capture records only the game's window, so Discord and notifications stay out.
  • Separate audio tracks for game, voice chat and your mic, each with its own volume.
  • Sessions: double-tap F9 for a whole match, and press H to mark what mattered.

Clip 04 Editor

Cut the montage without leaving Vice.

Drop clips on a timeline, trim and arrange them, add titles and transitions, then export an MP4. One switch makes it fit Discord's upload limit.

  • Crossfade
  • Fade to black
  • Fade to white
  • Dip to accent
  • Blur dissolve
  • Slide

Clip 05 New in 2.15

Show your keys.

A keyboard overlay for streams and clips, drawn over your game in your Vice colour. Pick a corner or drag it anywhere. Vice can also keep the keys with each clip, to show in the viewer or burn into an editor export.

WASDShiftSpace Go on, press them.

Or try a colour

Only the panel's own keys are read, and it is off until you turn it on. Needs X11, or KDE Plasma, Hyprland, Sway or Niri on Wayland.

Clip 06 Beyond games

Not only for games.

Vice records whatever is on your screen. Double-tap F9 to record until you press it again, for a tutorial, a bug report or a call you need to keep, and mark the moments that matter as you go.

Streaming

Run Vice beside OBS. The overlay puts your keys on stream, Game Capture keeps Discord off it, and F9 catches the moment you want to post later.

Everything else

Whole-screen sessions in one file, a screenshot key with a pen for marking things up, and Follow my mouse to record the monitor you are working on.

Clip 07 Library

Every clip, filed under the game it came from.

Vice knows 347 games and files each clip into a playlist for the one you were playing. Make your own playlists too, rename anything, and find any clip from the sidebar.

  • Trim keeps the original. Your selection saves as a new clip.
  • Your own playlists, with an emoji and a colour. Drag clips onto them.
  • Copy video puts the file itself on your clipboard, to paste anywhere.
  • Rich Presence shows the game you are clipping on Discord, with its icon.

And the rest of it.

Hotkeys anywhere
Read from the keyboard itself, so they work in fullscreen games and on every Wayland desktop.
Clip presets
Extra keys that each save their own length, from 5 seconds to 30 minutes.
Out of the way
Vice ignores its keys while an app that uses the same ones is focused.
System tray
Close the window and Vice keeps its buffer running from the tray.
Older NVIDIA cards
The Flatpak gpu-screen-recorder keeps them encoding on the GPU.
Sounds that tell you
Different tones for saved, failed, session and highlight, each one replaceable.
Five colour schemes
The Material You themes from the buttons at the top of this page.
Four languages
English, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese, translated by people who use Vice.
Nothing phones home
No account and no telemetry. The daily update check asks GitHub for a version number and sends nothing about you.
Open source
GPL-3.0. Read every line, change it, build your own.

Clip 08 Yours

Install it.
Press F9.

Arch and derivatives

From the AUR. Updates arrive with the rest of your system.

yay -S vice-clipper

Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, openSUSE

The installer sets up gpu-screen-recorder, Vice and its service, on derivatives too.

git clone https://github.com/splitshotz/Vice && cd Vice && ./install.sh

NixOS

Add the flake, add vice.nixosModules.default to your modules and set services.vice.enable = true.

inputs.vice.url = "github:splitshotz/Vice";

Setup details, troubleshooting and the full feature list are on GitHub.
